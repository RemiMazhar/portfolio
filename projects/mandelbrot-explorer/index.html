Mandelbrot Set Explorer

The Mandelbrot set is the most well known and studied non-self-similar fractal, a mathematical figure with an infinite level of detail. I made a program to explore visualizations of it, and generate videos of zooms into the set.

Accomplishments

Main features:

  • Render high resolution pictures and videos of very deep zooms in minutes
  • Fully optimized with GPU acceleration
  • Live exploration of the set
  • Support for many different rendering algorithms and colormaps through shaders

Skills

  • C++
  • CUDA
  • OpenGL
  • GUIs
  • ffmpeg
  • Complex Analysis
